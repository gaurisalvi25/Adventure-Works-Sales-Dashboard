# 🚴 Adventure Works Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes the sales performance of Adventure Works across regions, countries, and product subcategories.  
The project focuses on revenue growth, profit margins, customer distribution, and product performance analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track total sales and profit performance
- Analyze customer distribution by region
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare yearly sales and growth percentages
- Monitor YTD and previous year sales trends

---

## 📊 Key Features

### 🔹 KPI Indicators
- Total Sales: **29.36M**
- Total Profit: **12.08M**
- Total Customers: **18484**

### 🔹 Sales Performance Analysis
- Yearly Sales Comparison (2011–2015)
- Previous Year vs Current Year Sales
- YTD Sales Calculation
- % Change Analysis with visual indicators

### 🔹 Regional Insights
- Customers by Region (Donut Chart)
- Sales by Country (Bar Chart)
- Region & Country Filters

### 🔹 Product Analysis
- Top 5 / Top 10 Products by Sales
- Subcategory Sales Distribution
- Subcategory by Customer Count
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend Line

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query Editor
- DAX Measures
- Data Modeling (Fact & Dimension Tables)

---

## 🧮 DAX Measures Implemented
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Previous Year Sales
- YTD Sales
- % Growth
- Customer Count
- Dynamic Top N Filtering

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Road Bikes contribute over 50% of total revenue
- Significant growth observed in 2012 and 2013
- Slight decline detected in 2014
- North America region drives majority sales
- High customer concentration in Tires and Tubes category
